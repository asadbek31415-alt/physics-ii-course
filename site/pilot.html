About the Pilot

Physics II Interactive Course is in pilot release v0.9.6. It is being tested with university students so the course can be improved before a later stable release.

Pilot Goals

  • evaluate whether explanations are clear
  • learn which simulations help students most
  • identify errors or confusing lessons
  • improve course structure based on real student use

Feedback

Student feedback is voluntary. Please do not include sensitive personal information in feedback messages.

Students may report unclear explanations, errors, difficult sections, or examples and simulations that were especially helpful.
