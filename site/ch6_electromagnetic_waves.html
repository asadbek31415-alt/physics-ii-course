Electromagnetic Waves

In the previous chapter, we assembled the four pillars of Electrodynamics: Maxwell's Equations. Now, we explore their most profound mathematical consequence. In source-free regions, changing fields sustain each other in a self-propagating loop—an Electromagnetic Wave.

This chapter traces the path from Maxwell's Equations to the Wave Equation, through the transport of Energy & Momentum, and finally to the behavior of these fields at the boundaries of Matter.
