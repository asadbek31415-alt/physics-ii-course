Electric Fields in Matter

So far, we have studied electric fields in a vacuum. In this section, we explore how matter influences electrostatics. Matter comes in two main varieties: conductors and dielectrics (insulators).

We will examine how external fields induce charges on conductors or polarize the atoms within insulators. This leads to the concept of bound charges and necessitates the introduction of a new auxiliary field, the Electric Displacement (D).
