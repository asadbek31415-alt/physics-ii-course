Deep Dive: Chapter 6 Review

Key Concepts

Wave Equation Derived from Maxwell's Equations, predicting that EM waves travel at speed \(c = 1/\sqrt{\mu_0 \epsilon_0}\).
Transverse \(\mathbf{E}\) and \(\mathbf{B}\) are perpendicular to each other and to the direction of propagation \(\mathbf{k}\).
Energy Waves carry energy (Poynting vector \(\mathbf{S}\)) and momentum (\(\mathbf{g} = \mathbf{S}/c^2\)).
Boundaries Reflection and refraction governed by Fresnel equations and EM boundary conditions.
