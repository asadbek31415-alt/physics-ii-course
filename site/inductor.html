Simulator Insights

Current Slowdown: The inductor ($L$) opposes changes in current via self-inductance (Lenz's Law). The higher the inductance ($L$), the more resilience the circuit has to changes in current. When an EMF ($E$) is applied, the changing magnetic flux induces a Back EMF that resists the flow, causing current to ramp up gradually.

Resistive Scattering: In the resistor, electrons collide with the ion lattice (represented by the chaotic motion). This friction limits the final steady-state current to $I = E/R$.
