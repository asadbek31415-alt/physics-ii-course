Electrodynamics

So far, we have encountered Electrostatics and Magnetostatics. In this chapter, we begin the study of Electrodynamics, where electric and magnetic fields are no longer static but vary with time.

We begin by showing how conservation laws and slowly varying fields become the practical rules of circuit theory. We then explore Electromotive Force, Faraday's Law of Induction, and finally complete the full set of Maxwell's Equations.
