Magnetostatics

While electrostatics deals with stationary charges, Magnetostatics deals with stationary currents (steady flow of charge).

Here, the electric field is constant, but the moving charges generate a Magnetic Field (B). The fundamental laws governing this regime are the Biot-Savart Law (analogous to Coulomb's Law) and Ampère's Law (analogous to Gauss's Law). We also introduce the Vector Potential A, which plays a mathematical role similar to the scalar potential V.
