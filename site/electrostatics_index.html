Electrostatics

This section explores electrostatics—the study of electric charges that are stationary. The "static" condition is critical: if charges are moving, they produce magnetic fields and complicate the problem significantly. By assuming charges have no velocity, we can focus purely on the forces they exert on one another.

Ultimately, Coulomb’s Law and the principle of superposition constitute the complete physical input for this entire subject. The rest is the mathematical framework we build upon these two fundamental rules to solve for the force on any charge in any situation.
