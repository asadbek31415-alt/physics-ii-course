Deep Dive: Key Formulas & Concepts

Wave Properties

$$ c = \frac{1}{\sqrt{\mu_0 \epsilon_0}} \approx 3.00 \times 10^8 \text{ m/s} $$ $$ \omega = 2\pi f, \quad k = \frac{2\pi}{\lambda}, \quad c = \frac{\omega}{k} $$ $$ \mathbf{E} \perp \mathbf{B} \perp \mathbf{k}, \quad E_0 = c B_0 $$

Energy and Momentum

$$ \mathbf{S} = \frac{1}{\mu_0} (\mathbf{E} \times \mathbf{B}) \quad \text{(Poynting Vector)} $$ $$ I = \langle S \rangle = \frac{1}{2} c \epsilon_0 E_0^2 \quad \text{(Intensity)} $$ $$ P = I/c \quad \text{(Pressure, Absorber)} $$
