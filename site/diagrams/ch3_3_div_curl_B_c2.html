Derivation Steps

1. Symmetry & Loop

We choose a circular Amperian Loop (yellow ring) of radius $r$. Due to symmetry, the magnetic field magnitude $B$ is constant along this path.

$$ \oint \mathbf{B} \cdot d\mathbf{l} = B(2\pi r) $$

2. Enclosed Current

The loop is placed inside the donut structure. It encloses the central hole. Every wire turn ($N$ total) passes through the center of our loop.

Total Enclosed Current: $I_{\text{enc}} = N I$

3. Solve

Using Ampère's Law:

$$ B(2\pi r) = \mu_0 N I \implies B = \frac{\mu_0 N I}{2\pi r} $$

Magnetic Field Result

The magnetic field is confined entirely inside the toroid coil.

Visualization

The simulation uses particle brightness and speed to show field strength ($1/r$).

  • Inner Radius: Bright & Fast ($B$ is Strong)
  • Outer Radius: Dim & Slow ($B$ is Weak)
$$ B \propto \frac{1}{r} $$

Note: Outside the coil, $B \approx 0$.
