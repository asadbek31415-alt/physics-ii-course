Symmetry & Monopoles

Compare our asymmetric universe with one where magnetic charges exist.

Our Universe (Standard)
Full Symmetry (Monopoles)
$\nabla \cdot \mathbf{E} = \rho / \epsilon_0$
$\nabla \cdot \mathbf{B} = 0$
$\nabla \times \mathbf{E} = -\partial \mathbf{B} / \partial t$
$\nabla \times \mathbf{B} = \mu_0 \mathbf{J} + \dots$

In the symmetric case, Magnetic Charge acts just like electric charge, creating radial B-fields and "magnetic currents" that induce E-fields.
