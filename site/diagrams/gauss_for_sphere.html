Spherical Shell

0 R 5R
Condition: Outside ($r > R$)
Enclosed Charge ($Q_{enc}$): $Q$
Electric Field ($E$): $\frac{1}{4\pi\epsilon_0}\frac{Q}{r^2}$

Key Concept

Gauss's Law states: $\oint \vec{E} \cdot d\vec{A} = Q_{enc}/\epsilon_0$.

Inside ($r < R$): The Gaussian surface encloses NO charge. Thus, $E=0$.

Outside ($r > R$): It encloses ALL charge $Q$. It acts like a point charge at the center.
