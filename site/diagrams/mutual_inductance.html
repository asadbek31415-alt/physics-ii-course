The Ribbon Concept:
The Flux Rule $\mathcal{E} = -d\Phi_B/dt$ comes from calculating the change in magnetic flux as the loop moves.

The change in flux is exactly the magnetic field passing through the red "Ribbon" swept out by the wire.
