2. Momentum & Pressure

EM waves carry momentum. When light hits an object, it transfers this momentum, exerting a tiny but measurable force.
Perfect Absorption: $P = I/c$

Momentum is fully transferred as the surface stops the wave.

Perfect Reflection: $P = 2I/c$

The "bounce" (direction change $\Delta p$) doubles the total momentum transfer.
