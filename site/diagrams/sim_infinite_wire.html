Derivation Steps

1. Cylindrical Symmetry

The field must have the same magnitude at any point equidistant from the wire ($r$). We choose a circular Amperian loop (yellow) of radius $r$.

2. Evaluate Integral

Along this circle, $\mathbf{B}$ is always parallel to the path $d\mathbf{l}$.

$$ \oint \mathbf{B} \cdot d\mathbf{l} = \oint B \, dl = B(2\pi r) $$

3. Solve

The total enclosed current is simply $I$. Equating Ampère's Law:

$$ B(2\pi r) = \mu_0 I \implies B = \frac{\mu_0 I}{2\pi r} $$

The Infinite Wire

A long straight wire carrying current $I$ creates a magnetic field that circles the wire.

Visualization

The simulation shows how field strength decays as you move away from the wire ($1/r$).

  • Near Wire: Strong Field (Fast, Bright)
  • Far Away: Weak Field (Slow, Dim)
