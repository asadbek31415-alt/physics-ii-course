EXPERIMENTS

1. The Riemann Sum

Start with spaced dots, then fill the gaps. Watch the "bumpy" potential smooth out. This visualizes the limit as sum becomes integral.

V = Σ (q/r) → ∫ (dq/r)
2. Line Charge (λ)

Draw a long straight line. Notice the equipotentials form parallel cylinders. The potential decays slower than a point charge.

dq = λ dl'
3. Charged Shell (σ)

A cross-section of a uniformly charged spherical shell. The potential is constant inside, so the field (-∇V) is zero there.

Vin = constant
4. Volume Blob (ρ)

Fill in a solid shape. This represents volume density ρ. Far away, the complex shape's potential looks just like a single point charge.

V ≈ k Qtot / r
