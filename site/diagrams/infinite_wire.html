Derivation Steps

1. Cylindrical Symmetry

The field magnitude is constant at radius $r$. We choose a circular Amperian loop (yellow).

$$ \oint \mathbf{B} \cdot d\mathbf{l} = B(2\pi r) $$

2. Solve

$$ B(2\pi r) = \mu_0 I \implies B = \frac{\mu_0 I}{2\pi r} $$

The Infinite Wire

A long straight wire carrying current $I$ creates a magnetic field that circles the wire.

Visualization
