Key Concept

Charge is distributed uniformly throughout the volume ($\rho$).

Inside ($r < R$): $Q_{enc}$ grows with volume ($r^3$). The field increases linearly: $E \propto r$.

Outside ($r > R$): It acts like a point charge. The field decreases: $E \propto 1/r^2$.
