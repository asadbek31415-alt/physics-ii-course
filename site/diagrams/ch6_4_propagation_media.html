1. Propagation in Media

In a dielectric, constants $\epsilon_0$ and $\mu_0$ are replaced by the material's permittivity and permeability.
$v = \frac{1}{\sqrt{\epsilon\mu}} = \frac{c}{n}$
The Index of Refraction ($n$) determines how much the wave slows down. Since frequency $f$ is fixed:
  • $\lambda_{medium} = \lambda_0 / n$
  • $v_{medium} = c / n$
